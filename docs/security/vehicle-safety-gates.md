## Future gate sequence

1. State one unanswered question and show why preserved offline evidence cannot answer it.
2. Define the minimum observation or intervention and its exact data/output boundary.
3. For the fixed C-P profile only, use `C-P-MA-OWNER-ASSUMED-RISK` after adoption through protected `main`: Codex and Claude must review the exact hash-pinned profile, source and command manifest; separate informed owner authorization remains required. ECC review is recommended but optional when unavailable, and its actual unavailability must be recorded. Any ECC report that is provided must bind to the same commit and hashes and pass the same GO and finding checks. These are AI engineering reviews, not human safety certification. This exception applies only to C-P and does not change root, HondaHack, instrumentation or deployment gates.
4. Define parked conditions, responsible operator, stop conditions, interruption response, and post-test factory checks. For this C-P profile, use ON mode with the brake pedal not pressed and READY absent. Honda’s 2018 manual describes automatic engine start/stop during vehicle operation and defines READY as indicating the vehicle is ready to drive; this supports excluding READY as a conservative choice. EV-only expectation is not a sufficient control. Whether this head unit and existing ADB transport work in ON mode is unverified. If they do not, stop; do not move to READY or escalate privilege. The owner must be outdoors, in Park with parking brake set, and stop if READY appears, the engine starts, any warning/abnormality occurs, power state changes, or factory operation is uncertain.
5. After adoption, exact-source reviews and verification of the clean, current protected-main checkout, obtain separate owner approval for the exact run, vehicle power state and serial. Other vehicle activities retain qualified independent safety review plus separate exact owner approval. This roadmap is not authorization.
6. Stop on any unexpected target, permission, output, warning, process, connectivity, or identity state. Do not broaden scope or retry outside approval.
7. Retain raw evidence privately and publish only a sanitized, source-pinned result.

For C-P only, the fixed PR #118 eight-phase profile is eligible for the owner-assumed-risk multi-agent path; it is read-only host diagnostics around owner-guided ordinary CarPlay connect/disconnect and factory Navigation selection transitions. Its operations, exact serial requirement, per-command and aggregate time/output ceilings, owner-confirmed Park/parking-brake/power-state conditions, and private evidence boundary are immutable within this category. Stop without retry on unexpected behavior. The fixed manifest permits 135 command entries, with summed per-command ceilings of 2,425 seconds and 158,400,512 output bytes; these are worst-case bounds, not expected collection size or a safety finding. Diagnostic activity can consume head-unit resources, expose identifiers or personal data in raw output, perturb connectivity/timing, or trigger unforeseen target software behavior; factory UI transitions change active infotainment state temporarily; read-only ADB intent does not prove zero side effects. The owner accepts these residual risks; the AI reviews do not certify the procedure safe. If the ON/non-READY state fails to support diagnostics, display behavior, target-side changes, or recovery are uncertain, stop or seek the separate qualified-human review path. CL-INT-01 cannot begin until receiver and display contracts, lab acceptance, and its own safety review pass. CL-VT-01 additionally needs a reversible procedure and separate exact test authorization. Never test while driving or deliberately generate unsafe vehicle warnings. If warning priority or restoration cannot be proved, record NO-GO.

The owner-directed category never covers privileged `su`/root reads, `adb root`, SuperSU invocation, instrumentation, process attachment, HondaHack, display control/modification, firmware, CAN, warning handling, deployment, or driving. Each remains a separately scoped and reviewed activity.
