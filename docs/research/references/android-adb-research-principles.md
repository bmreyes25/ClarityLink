# Android ADB research principles

The cited [0xCodez workflow reference](https://x.com/0xCodez/status/2111875629473743224) is process inspiration: verify target identity and owner authority, capture a baseline, scope each action, explain and approve changes, timestamp outcomes, verify results, preserve rollback details, and stop on surprises.

Its head-unit package cleanup, DNS changes, animation settings, browser installation, taps/key events, and entertainment launches do not answer ClarityLink's Type111 or Display 1 contracts. They are not a Honda procedure and are outside current scope. No vehicle-executable ADB script is included. Any future observation needs its own approved protocol, independent safety review, and explicit authorization. No Honda or vehicle operation occurred in CL-R13-A.
