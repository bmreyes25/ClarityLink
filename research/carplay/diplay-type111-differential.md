## Consequence for ClarityLink

This is strong external support for testing the generic second-display concept and for specifying separate display identity, view geometry, stream-111 response ownership, and UI-control questions. It does not validate a Honda response. The minimum hypothetical stock-first Honda experiment has two distinct negotiation sites: (1) optional second-display descriptor in the `/info` path, if Honda/iPhone testing shows it is needed; (2) any required `enabledFeatures` and Type111 response extension in the successful Setup response before its existing serializer. At both sites preserve Honda Type110 and audio exactly. The present callsite model covers only site (2).

No cryptographic routine from DiPlay is selected for Honda. In particular, the similar 128-byte framing family does not make the body cipher, key labels, nonce rules, or per-stream KDF compatible.
