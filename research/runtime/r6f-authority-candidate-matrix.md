**Selection:** LIVI + a compatible, user-owned CPC200-CCPA is the sole primary acquisition target because it pairs a documented real MFi endpoint with a full Mac-capable receiver stack that can be adapted. This is not yet a ClarityLink control provider: do not claim T0 or solved authentication until provenance/authorization is verified and the LIVI stack hands a live authenticated control channel into ClarityLink. OCBM is a credible hardware authority but its published ownership split keeps `/info` and SETUP inside the box, so it does not win.
