# R7C authentication boundary

Default Android construction supplies `UnavailableAuthenticationAuthority` and fails closed. Synthetic authority is reachable only through the explicit JNI lab-mode argument for an offline harness. No call to Honda `jmcs`, authenticated-session handoff, or `/dev/i2c-2` reuse is implemented.

Provider seams can later represent genuine external authority, project-owned lawful MFi hardware, or licensed service without selecting or emulating one. `REAL_MFI_AUTH`, `REAL_IPHONE_INFO`, `REAL_IPHONE_SETUP`, `REAL_TYPE110_SECURITY`, `REAL_TYPE111_SECURITY`, and `REAL_TYPE111_FRAMING` remain `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`.
