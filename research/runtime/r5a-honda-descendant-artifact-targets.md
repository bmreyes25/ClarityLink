### Acquisition discipline

Prioritize publicly downloadable owner manuals, release notes, regulatory filings, patents, developer documentation, and artifacts affirmatively licensed for analysis. A public update PDF is not the update payload. Private owner-supplied binaries require provenance, lawful access, license review, and local-only handling; do not solicit credentials, bypass access controls, or commit proprietary payloads. @ECC review: no marketing claim, model similarity, route-guidance behavior, or third-party binary is promoted to Honda Type111 evidence.
