The API17/ARMv7 Test A native artifact is built, audited, and offline-tested. Its mandatory dependency/import unknown count is zero. Test E remains safety/evidence blocked, Test F remains an observation plan and factory restoration is unproven, Test G retains the R7D 14.405 FPS emulator result with performance unresolved, and Test H remains authority-required.
