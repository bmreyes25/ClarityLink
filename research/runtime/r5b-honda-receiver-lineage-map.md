# R5B — Honda/Acura receiver lineage map

Public-source research snapshot: 2026-10-04. This is a lineage map, not a claim that all CarPlay-equipped vehicles share a receiver. The public `librick/ic1101` project states coverage for most 2016–2021 Civic head units (with trim exceptions) and describes Tegra 3, ARMv7, Android 4.2.2, and Mitsubishi Andromeda. Exact per-model receiver binaries remain uninspected here.

## Source boundary

The Civic family row derives from a public reverse-engineering project’s stated scope and technical overview, plus its version table. It is not independent validation of every trim/year. The official Honda update portal is public but requires a vehicle-generated file; this milestone did not use a vehicle. Public cluster/navigation descriptions establish semantics only, not Type111 video transport. @ECC finding: top candidate family is promising, but no analyzed descendant binary.
