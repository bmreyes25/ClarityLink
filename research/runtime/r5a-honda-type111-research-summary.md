# R5A — Honda Type111 recovery research summary

## Findings

- Later Honda/Civic and Accord families are promising **research targets** because official Honda material describes CarPlay-era instrument-cluster/driver-information-interface route guidance on some configurations. This establishes, at most, a semantic navigation-data path. It does not identify receiver implementation or prove a second H.264 stream.
- No publicly/legally accessible Honda/Acura descendant receiver binary with Type111-positive parsing, response construction, second-screen setup, security, listener, and teardown was found in this milestone.
- No additive receiver API or supported receiver plugin loader was found in the preserved Honda service/native evidence. R3C remains controlling for `jmcs` and Type111: `R3C_NO_SAFE_RUNTIME_EXTENSION_PATH_FOUND` / `ABANDON_RUNTIME_INTERPOSITION_UNTIL_NEW_EVIDENCE`.
- Official secondary-display evidence is strong at the generic Apple/platform level but transport-agnostic on Honda's receiver side. Legacy AES Type111 is strong external prior art, not Honda linkage. Current iOS two-screen lab evidence is also not Honda evidence.
- Honda cluster route metadata, iAP2 route guidance, manuals, public product language, and visible cluster UI are all non-decisive for secondary H.264 transport.

## Decision

**R5A:** `R5A_HONDA_TYPE111_DESCENDANT_PROMISING_BUT_NO_BINARY` — descendant families merit lawful public-artifact cataloging, but no Honda Type111-positive descendant binary was found. This does not reopen R3C or authorize runtime work.

**Next evidence step:** `GO_FOR_MORE_PUBLIC_ARTIFACT_RESEARCH`, limited to public manuals/update metadata and provenance/licensing research that could identify a legitimate, versioned receiver artifact. No private binary acquisition or vehicle step follows automatically.
