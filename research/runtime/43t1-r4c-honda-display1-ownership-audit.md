**Ownership conclusion:** the only **observed** Display 1 render owner is Honda's externaldisplay process. A separately owned app window remains a plausible Android API-17 hypothesis, but no Honda artifact proves admission, stacking, safe physical visibility, or coexistence with Honda interrupt/warning roots. No service replacement, injection or runtime change is justified by this audit.
