## Triage

The best next study candidate is **lifecycle cleanup ownership and event coverage**, limited to existing repository artifacts and static closure. Reports establish a Honda finalizer path, but do not establish a project-child subscription or complete failure coverage. This is worth resolving as an ownership prerequisite; it is not a presently usable Setup seam.

The existing wrapper question has substantial historical static closure: the Setup serializer call is a direct local call, and the 43M wrapper is `MODEL_ONLY`. The serializer-adjacent interval is a real static boundary, but entry requires a prohibited redirect. Callback tables are replacement records rather than evidenced observer lists. Preload and external-process approaches fail the safety boundary or lack Honda-specific evidence.

No candidate authorizes or justifies runtime work. All candidates requiring inline patching, thread-stop assumptions, root/su, ptrace, `/proc/<pid>/mem`, APK install, startup persistence, block-device writes, CAN writes, USB injection, or HondaHack persistent mutation are `REJECT_UNSAFE`.
