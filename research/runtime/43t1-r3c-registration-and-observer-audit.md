## Determination

**Append-only session registration found: NO.** No recovered `AddObserver`, `RegisterHandler`, `AddListener`, subscriber list, notification fan-out, callback chain, session registry insertion/removal API, or plugin/provider registry lets a project consumer coexist with Honda's consumer for Setup and cleanup.

The stock streams array demonstrates that multiple **data records** may be appended to an individual Setup response. It does not demonstrate additive runtime registration, observer coexistence, callback reachability, session-lifetime ownership, or permission to alter the graph. Honda's callback records instead show whole-record copies and a single global event slot. Generic device-manager multi-candidate dispatch is not evidence of a Honda session extension: no session-keyed Setup/cleanup observer contract or non-displacing winning-handler chain was recovered.

No API absence is claimed outside the preserved evidence and reviewed paths. Unknown remains unknown; the architecture decision is based on the absence of a demonstrated supported path, not a proof about every possible firmware function.
