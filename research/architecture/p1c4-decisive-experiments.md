### Conditional future C lab protocol, if authoritative admission is found

**Purpose:** test simultaneous sessions, not Type111 decoding or Honda compatibility. **Equipment:** one user-owned iPhone with recorded iOS build, two isolated non-Honda receivers with recorded software hashes, dedicated wired and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth transports, and private redacted event logging. **Credentials:** two separately legitimate, authorized accessory authorities; the previous shared PlayPort experimental identity is excluded. **Preconditions:** first obtain a source-supported reason to expect concurrent sessions; review pairing/network configuration and audio safety; authorize this exact lab separately. No vehicle is involved.

**Bounded sequence and evidence:** start receiver 1 and record a pseudonymous session-generation token, its authorized identity category, active transport, control ownership, Type110 frame continuity and audio/call/Siri route state. Start receiver 2 once, with a different authorized identity; for at most 60 seconds record whether it authenticates, whether its distinct session/control transport becomes active, whether receiver 1 keeps receiving Type110, whether audio/call/Siri ownership changes, and the disconnect/close events of both. Record no raw session IDs, keys, credentials, payloads, routes or private device identifiers. Both receivers must clean up independently after the bounded observation.

**Stop immediately** on unexpected first-receiver teardown, audio/call/Siri takeover, identity ambiguity, credential error, uncontrolled reconnection, transport anomaly, or missing redaction. **Success for this lab profile** requires two concurrent authenticated sessions with distinct authority/transport identities, uninterrupted first-receiver Type110 and expected audio/control ownership for the observation window. **Classify failure separately:** second session refused; second attempt hands off/ends first; both connect but ownership conflicts; transport/authentication uncertain; timeout/incomplete evidence. Even success would not establish that stock Honda can coexist with a second receiver or that Display 1 can show its output.
