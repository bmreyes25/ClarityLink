# P1-C4 — independent receiver coexistence feasibility

**Decision: `P1_C4_CONDITIONAL_COEXISTENCE_REQUIRES_PROOF`.** A one-session, stock-primary, separately delegated secondary endpoint is architecturally coherent **if** Honda exposes an authorized session-bound control/media/security contract; [P1-C2](p1c2-jmcs-additive-extension-audit.md) found none in the reviewed artifacts. Two independent receivers sharing one phone/session or running simultaneous sessions have no proven admission/identity contract. The custom receiver is a useful lab oracle, but cannot presently be recommended beside the stock Honda receiver. This bounded result does not limit what custom software can implement after a real ingress boundary is established. No vehicle, iPhone, USB, or network operation was performed.

## Provenance and official model

This work reused [P1-C1](p1c1-stock-session-ownership.md), [P1-C2](p1c2-jmcs-additive-extension-audit.md), [P1-C3](p1c3-iap2-route-guidance-ingress.md), [R6D](../runtime/r6d-authenticated-session-boundary-audit.md), [stock primary path](../carplay/primary-screen-end-to-end.md), and [Step 42A](../../step-reports/42a-type111-display-stream-correlation.md). The original binaries were read only.

[Apple WWDC16 Part 2](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2016/723/) describes the CarPlay Communication Plug-in, IP link, audio and video frameworks as one vehicle-system integration. [WWDC19](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2019/252/) says iOS 13 supports multiple H.264 streams for cluster content with R15 communication plug-in APIs. [WWDC23](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10150/) distinguishes additional navigation UI video from iAP2 guidance metadata. These public presentations show **one capable vehicle integration with multiple outputs**; they do not publish Type110/111 numeric protocol guarantees, a two-receiver session-sharing ABI, or Honda compatibility. The full technical specification is MFi-restricted; simultaneous independent sessions for one iPhone and receiver-side delegation remain unverified. [Apple WWDC17 wireless guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2017/717/) tells a vehicle to check for any active CarPlay session, including the same device, before reconnecting and explains that iPhone chooses a wired or wireless transport. This strongly weighs against treating simultaneous Honda wired plus separate wireless CarPlay as a supported design, without proving universal iOS refusal.

## Stock and custom ownership

The stock `jmcs` owns USB/iAP2, factory MFi authentication, AirPlay control and session state, the Type110 listener/security/frame path and stock audio/controls; its `SETUP` skips Type111. Proxy screen/auth/audio callbacks are process-local singletons. Honda's actual `CarPlay Screen` device winner and final sink remain unresolved, but no external authenticated secondary handoff was found. Existing Binder navigation callbacks are two status booleans, not authentic maneuver content. The [resource matrix](p1c4-receiver-topologies.json) marks each topology's owner or conflict; **no resource is silently dual-owned**.

ClarityLink's [receiver session](../../src/claritylink-jmcs/claritylink_jmcs/session.py) owns its authentication provider, generation-bound handoff, `/info`, `SETUP`, and transport. It is a complete receiver/lab oracle, not a plug-in to Honda's session. The [native receiver API](../../native/include/claritylink/receiver.hpp), [transport/parser](../../src/claritylink-transport/receiver_core.py), [negotiation model](../../src/claritylink-negotiation/type111_setup_contract.py), [project lifecycle registry](../../src/carplay-session-model/project_lifecycle.py), and [Android Surface sink](../../native/platform/android/android_surface_sink.hpp) are reusable only after a valid source boundary exists. The project has an offline Type110/111 model, session-generation checks, H.264 handling and separate rendering; host tests and synthetic replay are **MODEL_ONLY**. Android API17, output admission, and final Display 1 integration remain separate gates.

The preserved [PlayPort 43P lab observation](../lab/current-ios-type111-observation.md) shows **one experimental receiver** negotiating Type111 before Type110, distinct connection IDs/ports and simultaneous video in one lab session. Its shared experimental identity and modern crypto profile are not Honda credentials or Honda interoperability evidence. [LIVI](../runtime/r6g-livi-carplay-architecture-map.md), [DiPlay/xcertplay](../carplay/honda-xcertplay-diplay-playport-differential.md) and [MHI2](../carplay/mhi2-porting-lessons.md) are external prior art for one receiver's multiple streams or stock-first hooks. They do not demonstrate two receivers sharing Honda's phone session. MHI2's interception/key observation is excluded by this milestone's restrictions. No public project reviewed supplies a supported Honda no-APK session delegation contract.

## Topology verdicts

| ID | Ownership and result | Exact blocker or bounded use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Honda controls one authenticated session; ClarityLink receives delegated secondary media. **Conditional.** | Requires a Honda `/info`/SETUP contribution, Type111 endpoint and security handoff, generation/close notifications, and external admission. None is proven. |
| B | Honda and ClarityLink each claim a receiver session over one iPhone connection. **Unproven.** | USB/iAP2 admission, independent authorized identity, session multiplexing and control/audio isolation are unknown; raw session pointers cannot cross processes. |
| C | Honda wired and ClarityLink wireless simultaneously. **Unsupported as final design on present evidence.** | Apple guidance selects one active transport/session for reconnection; no second identity, coexistence or audio ownership proof. |
| D | Honda controls session, ClarityLink hosts only Type111 TCP media. **Conditional subset of A.** | Honda must advertise the display, accept Type111 SETUP and return an endpoint; it must securely bind per-stream media to that endpoint. Listener alone is inert. |
| E | Separate ClarityLink service via existing Android/vendor IPC. **Unproven.** | Reviewed Binder/native endpoints expose status/UI or generic channels, not authenticated frames/session/security. No-APK permission is unverified. |
| F | Complete ClarityLink lab receiver. **Lab oracle.** | Proves custom negotiation/transport behavior for its own identity/profile, not coexistence with stock Honda. |
| G | Independent phone-to-ClarityLink companion data channel. **Additional data path required.** | May support ClarityLink's own navigation with an authorized iOS companion; it does not automatically mirror Apple Maps/Waze's active route. |

No topology H with a concrete authorized transport, authentication owner, session identity and lifecycle was found. The [session ownership contract](p1c4-session-ownership-contract.md) separates receiver control obligations from independently implementable media work. [Decisive experiments](p1c4-decisive-experiments.md) are future proposals only.

## Suitability and next step

**Best conditional architecture:** A/D, one stock Honda session and an explicitly delegated secondary media endpoint, because it could preserve stock Type110/auth/audio/control ownership. It is **not deployment-ready**: the necessary Honda interface and Apple acceptance are absent from reviewed evidence. A separate full receiver can remain an isolated lab oracle. A second simultaneous receiver is not a justified fallback. P1-C5 should compare these evidence gates; it was not started here.
